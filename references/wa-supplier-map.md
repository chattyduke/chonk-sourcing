# WA / Perth supplier map (warm leads)

Starting leads for a Perth WA food operator, gathered 2026-06. Verify pricing on contact —
treat figures as benchmarks, not quotes. Confirm WA-deliverability before relying on any
interstate option.

## One-stop foodservice distributors (best first stop for a small operator)

- **Bidfood Malaga** — depot in Malaga (Perth NE suburbs). Account-based, apply online,
  tailored per-branch pricing; frozen, chilled, dry, dairy, packaging on one invoice.
  Same-day/6-day delivery in metro. Best single account for most of a smoothie goods list.
- **PFD Food Services (Perth + regional WA)** — 12,000+ lines, nationwide with WA branches
  (incl. Kalgoorlie, Esperance). Quote against Bidfood to play them off.
- **Superior Foods** — national foodservice, trade web accounts.

Use these to benchmark: frozen fruit, peanut butter, yoghurt (10kg buckets), cocoa, oats,
packaging — all obtainable here at trade pricing.

## Frozen fruit / berries (Perth-local)

- **FrozBerries WA / HarvestimeWA** — U2/9 Industry Street, Malaga WA;
  (08) 9248 2673; sales@frozberrieswa.com.au. This is Harvestime's WA arm (resells Harvestime
  / Elgin Organics brands). **Local pickup kills interstate freight** — ask for a volume +
  pickup rate, not just delivered.
- **EVOO Quality Foods (WA)** — WA wholesaler stocking Harvestime frozen berries.
- Benchmark both against Costco's frozen berry $/kg — club pricing is often competitive.

## Interstate importers (reach Perth only via WA distributors — usually NOT cheaper after freight)

- **Harvestime** (importer) — 29 Hope St, Melrose Park NSW 2114; (02) 9482 3411; Mon–Fri
  8–4. Account-based, no published MOQ. WA enquiries route to FrozBerries WA. Direct-from-NSW
  to Perth = back to the local arm + freight.
- **The Berry Man** (importer) — Brookvale NSW; (02) 9907 9600; info@berryman.com.au.
  $125 min order via Ordermentum; free delivery Sydney metro only; serves Perth through its
  WA distributor; interstate freight ~$250–500/drop. Strong for acai specifically.
- **Purple Foods**, **Seriously Healthy**, **Chefs Pantry** — bulk/IQF importers (VIC/NSW);
  confirm WA freight before assuming a saving.

## Dairy (WA-local)

- **Brownes Dairy** — WA dairy since 1886; wholesale milk + yoghurt across WA via an
  exclusive distributor network. Milk in bottles ~$1.30–1.60/L delivered (near Costco's
  ~$1.43/L — milk barely compresses). Pergal (bag-in-box ~13.6L) is the only lower tier;
  needs a dispenser/chiller and ~48h post-open window — usually impractical for a trailer.
- **Bulk Greek yoghurt** (10kg buckets, WA-deliverable via foodservice account): Farmers
  Union Euro Style (~$3.78/kg trade via Provista), Procal Authentic Greek 10kg, George's,
  Anando (min 1 × 10kg). Coconut yoghurt stays $8–15/kg regardless — does not follow.

## Protein (whey)

- **WPC floor** ~$30–34/kg for natural/unflavoured in 20kg sacks (Professional Whey, Pure
  Product Australia/Fonterra base, Wholesale Nutrition, Bulk Nutrients NWPC). Per-scoop
  retail (~$31.7/kg) is already near this — the lever is buying 20kg sacks + reformulating,
  not finding a cheaper whey seller.

## Fresh produce (banana etc.)

- **Perth Market, Canning Vale (Market City)** — central wholesale fruit & veg market;
  banana in 13kg cartons at trade pricing (~$1.20–1.80/kg vs ~$2.50/kg grocery). Access via
  a market wholesaler or a cafe fruit-&-veg delivery run.

## Packaging (custom-print cups)

- Lever is MOQ scaling + going factory-direct on the print rather than a local middleman;
  current bundle ~$0.34/serve is a reasonable starting point.
